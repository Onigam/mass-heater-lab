# Water, fire and smoke: what follows the model and what is decoration

There is no open water in this lab. Fire, gases and smoke take its place and follow the same rule (`lab-quality.md` §3): where the physics says something the picture obeys it, and what is decoration is listed here.

| Thing                         | Reads                                              | Follows the model                                                                                                                                                                        | Decoration                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The flame                     | `flameLength`, `flameStrength`, `flameClock`       | its length along the gases' path, its side (negative: back up the feed and out of its mouth), its strength, its clock with the slow clock stopped                                        | the shape of the tongues and licks, the pattern that runs along them                                                         |
| The firelight                 | `flameStrength`, `emberGlow`                       | its strength                                                                                                                                                                             | a gentle variation of ±7 % on the flame's clock, never a strobe                                                              |
| The glow in the feed's slot   | `flameStrength`, `emberGlow`                       | its strength: from the room it is all you see of a fire drawn down into the tunnel                                                                                                       | its soft shape                                                                                                               |
| The embers                    | `emberGlow`                                        | how bright they are                                                                                                                                                                      | where the lumps lie                                                                                                          |
| The sticks                    | `feedLevel`                                        | how many stand and how far they have burned; their feet keep to the tunnel's floor                                                                                                       | their lean, their char line                                                                                                  |
| The lid                       | `params.feedCover`, `params.lidAfterFire`, `phase` | the share of the mouth it hides; it stops at the sticks, which `feedCover` counts in (research: one brick hides 11 of 18 cm), and closes fully when the fire is out if the model says so | none                                                                                                                         |
| The ash                       | `ash`                                              | the height of the bed, a little                                                                                                                                                          | its relief                                                                                                                   |
| The riser's liner             | `linerT`                                           | a glow, only over about 480 °C (the eye's own threshold)                                                                                                                                 | none                                                                                                                         |
| The gases in the section      | `pathT` by station                                 | the tint and thickness of the gas in the riser, the gap and the fall, from the model's temperature there                                                                                 | none                                                                                                                         |
| The dots along the path       | `gasFlow`, `pathT`                                 | direction; speed = mass flow over density at the local temperature and over the local section (they rush in the riser, slow in the fall, crawl in the duct); colour from the temperature | their scatter across the channel. A visual cap of 2.2 m/s at ×1 (`GAS_VISUAL_CAP`), reached only in the riser at a full burn |
| The beads (« Flux »)          | `pathP` by station                                 | blue under suction, red when the inside pushes out, sized by how much                                                                                                                    | none                                                                                                                         |
| Smoke in the room             | `smokeInRoom`, `leakShare`                         | the amount, and where it comes from: the feed's mouth, or the drum's lid when the model has a leak                                                                                       | the puffs' shape, a slow rise                                                                                                |
| Steam at the kettle           | `drumLidT`                                         | starts when the lid is over boiling, 105 to 140 °C                                                                                                                                       | all of its look; the kettle is a warm habit, not a model                                                                     |
| Smoke at the cap              | `smokeAtCap`                                       | the amount: unburned fuel                                                                                                                                                                | the wind, the shape of the plume                                                                                             |
| Steam at the cap              | `steamAtCap`                                       | the amount: only water                                                                                                                                                                   | the wind, the shape                                                                                                          |
| The neighbour's smoke         | nothing                                            | none                                                                                                                                                                                     | all of it: the same wood on a slow fire, a contrast the part's sheet explains                                                |
| The needle of the flue's dial | `flueInT`                                          | 0 to 300 °C over 270°, at the eye's pace                                                                                                                                                 | no figure on the dial                                                                                                        |
| The needle of the probe       | `benchT` at its tip                                | 15 to 65 °C over 270°                                                                                                                                                                    | no figure on the dial                                                                                                        |
| The clock on the wall         | `hour`                                             | its two hands                                                                                                                                                                            | none                                                                                                                         |

Every motion takes `view.slowMo`, and `dt` = 0 moves nothing: a film can replay a frame.
