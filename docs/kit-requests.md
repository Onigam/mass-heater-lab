# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.5.1 lacks, from the physics and panel work of Mass Heater Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. Nothing here was worked around by patching the kit; each item says what the lab did instead. Items already asked by another lab say so: this lab is one more user.

1. **`sim/thermal` into the kit: this lab is its second user, and it extended it.** `src/sim/thermal/` is a copy of the sand lab's, plus what `docs/physics.md` §2 lists: `draft.ts` (`stackDraft`, `velocityHead`, `solveLoopFlow`), `radiation.ts` (`radiationConductance`, `enclosedEmissivity`, `naturalFilm`), `ramp.ts` (`heatStops`, `heatRgb`, `heatRampGlsl`), and in `exchange.ts` the two pieces of `tubeFlow` as their own functions (`tubeFriction`, `tubeNusselt`, `RE_LAMINAR`, same results). Nothing removed, nothing renamed. Pure, no lab name, tested. The two copies have now diverged: whoever moves it should take this one. The solar heater's thermosiphon wants `solveLoopFlow`.
2. **A false-colour legend (sand lab's request 8).** This lab's thermal view needs a `heatLegend(min, max, label)` overlay that says once « false colour », with the ramp of the core. The ramp is ready and fixed in the data (`THERMAL_RAMP` in `src/sim/machine.ts`, 15 to 65 °C). No workaround yet: asked before wave 2, the 3D role decides. The sand lab drew its scale as a prop in the world; a film shows no words, so a kit overlay hidden in a film is the better answer.
3. **A persistent safety banner.** One line, dismissible but returning, for the fire and gas labs (this one, the biodigester, the gasifier). `GuideStep.warning` and the Sources panel exist; nothing shows across all modes. The research's §7.2 text is ready. Until then the panel says once that a model cannot say a heater is safe, and `about()` is the lead's.
4. **A time slider that scrubs a day.** The engine reads a settled day from a table of 96 frames: `sim.set({ hour })` moves the whole machine, and live play goes on from there. The panel uses the plain `slider` with an `hh:mm` format, which works, and it lives in the Simulation panel only. Asked: a time scrubber in the kit's bottom strip beside the clock, so that it stays under the finger in Explore too, where the thermal view is. Same for the bioclimatic house and the solar heater.
5. **Speed labels (sand lab's request 4).** The strip writes `×21600`, which says nothing to a visitor and is wide on a phone. This lab has « a minute a second », « an hour a second », « six hours a second ». Asked: `speeds` entries with a label.
6. **Tags on a figure (sand lab's request 6).** Every number shown carries `†` or `*`: the panel appends it in `<small>`. A `readout({ label, value, unit, tag })` widget with a legend once per panel would make it cheap, and it would let a long label wrap instead of being cut at 319 px (« Banc, côté chaud » was, so this lab shortened it).
7. **Chart ticks and a band.** `LineChart` draws only its first and last x labels, so a 24 h chart reads « 00:00 … 00:00 » with no 6 h, 12 h, 18 h. Asked: `xTicks?: number[]`. And a shaded band around a series (`band: { low, high }`): the CO in the flue is a range of one to nine, shown here as text. Also shaded spans on the x axis, for « the fire is on » on a day chart.
8. **Part categories that a lab can name (sand lab's request 9).** This lab's are `fire`, `gas`, `store`, `fuel`, `safety`, `air`, `upkeep`. For the data role, not used by the physics.
9. **`section` that starts closed, `num`, the fake DOM (Mill Lab's and the sand lab's).** `src/panels/test-dom.ts` here is a straight copy of the sand lab's, `num` another version, and the panel closes each section by hand.

## From the 3D of the same lab (wave 2)

10. **A landscape that is not called `site`.** `Stage.setActive` and `frameAll` decide a world's near and far planes, its lowest camera and its authored framing by `id === 'site'` (or `'intake'`). This lab's setting is `homestead`, by the research's frozen ids, so it would draw 60 m of land. Asked: `SceneModule.landscape?: boolean`, or `camera?: { near, far, maxPolar }`. What the lab does meanwhile, both one line and both to delete when the kit answers: `scene.onBeforeRender` sets the camera's planes (`src/three/homestead/scene.ts`), and `onFrame` in `src/lab.ts` holds `controls.maxPolarAngle` at 0.48π in that world. The overview of this world is framed on its parts, as a device's is.
11. **A world's own tool in the toolbar.** The thermal view is this lab's « aha » and the kit's toolbar has no slot for it: Section, Flows, X-ray, Labels and Slow-mo are the kit's. So the switch lives in the Simulation panel, with the 24 h slider, and on the part sheets of what stores heat (`partActions`, a kit hook used for the purpose). In Explore it takes two clicks. Asked: `SceneDef.tools?: { label: L; active(): boolean; toggle(): void }[]`, drawn with the kit's other buttons. The bioclimatic house and the solar heater will want the same.
12. **The time scrubber, again (request 4).** The thermal view is only half of the idea without an hour under the finger in Explore: the slider is in the Simulation panel. This is the owner's first complaint waiting to happen.
13. **The legend (request 2).** Done for now the sand lab's way, a bar on a stand in the world with two of the kit's labels; it shows only with the thermal view, by the group's `visible`, which `CSS2DRenderer` respects. The kit's `heatLegend` would replace it and a film would not have to filter it. Asked: a `Hotspot.visible?: () => boolean` too, so that a label's condition needs no group of its own.
14. **The camera in `update`.** The walls of the room step aside when they stand between the eye and the room. `update(state, dt, view)` does not know the camera, so a shader discards them (`roomSide`, one program for all the walls) and each mesh's `raycast` reads `rc.camera`. With `view.camera` a world could hide them instead, and the shadow pass would agree. Asked: `ViewOpts.camera`.
15. **An intersection of clipping planes in the picking (the sand lab's request).** `Stage.cast` skips a hit if any plane of the material says the point is cut. With `clipIntersection` that refuses most of the machine; `GuardedPlane` in `src/three/section.ts` overrides `distanceToPoint` to answer for the pair. Asked: the kit reads `material.clipIntersection`.
16. **Shared 3D helpers.** `merge.ts`, `detail.ts`, `common.ts`, `homestead/sky.ts`, `homestead/snow.ts`, half of `textures.ts` and the spruces of `homestead/vegetation.ts` are copies of the sand lab's, which copied Mill's. New here, wanted by others: `Puffs` (smoke and steam as soft billboards on a clock, one draw call a plume), `Flame` (axial billboard ribbons along a path, the biodigester and the gasifier will want it), `GasFlow` (dots at the speed of a mass flow over a section), `ClipSet` and `Caps` (a section in steps), `Field` and `ThermalView` (false colour on any material through an attribute).
17. **Compile ahead of the first frame.** Each world has about 100 programs (materials × clipping × shadows). The first visit to a world stalls for seconds on a machine that draws everything else at 60 frames; `warmLater` starts after the first frame and only for the world in view. Asked: `renderer.compileAsync` before a world is shown, with the frame kept on the old world until it has resolved.

## From the integration of the same lab

18. **The Plans panel does not follow the sheet.** `drawing` is not in the keys that redraw the side panel (`panelKeys` in `dist/ui/app.js`), so that after choosing another sheet the list still highlights the first one and the card below it still gives the first one's title, description and parts. The sheet itself changes. Every lab of kit 0.5.1 has this: seen here at 1280, 768 and 390 px, no workaround in the lab. Asked: `drawing` in `panelKeys`.
